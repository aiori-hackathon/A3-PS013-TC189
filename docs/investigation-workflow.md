# Investigation Workflow

1. Define the incident scope and create an investigation.
2. Collect evidence from authorized systems.
3. Preserve and hash all evidence.
4. Parse and normalize timestamps without guessing unknown values.
5. Review collection gaps and parsing failures.
6. Verify evidence integrity.
7. Reconstruct the timeline.
8. Review cross-system correlations and their explanations.
9. Dismiss false positives with reasons.
10. Generate and archive a report.
11. Document limitations and unresolved questions.
